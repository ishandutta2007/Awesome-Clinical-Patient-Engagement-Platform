# Awesome-Clinical-Patient-Engagement-Platform

# Top Patient Engagement Platforms (Clinical) Ecosystem
**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on Clinical Trial Patient Engagement, eConsent, eCOA/ePRO, Decentralized Trials (DCT), Telehealth & Participant Retention*
**Last updated: September 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Clinical Patient Engagement**. These systems support participants throughout clinical trials—eConsent, electronic clinical outcome assessments (eCOA/ePRO), televisits, reminders, payments, and retention tools used in decentralized and hybrid studies.

**Examples** include Medable, THREAD Research, Science 37, Signant Health, Clario, Castor, MyTrials, ObvioHealth, Reify Health, and YPrime (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Production clinical patient engagement and DCT platforms are almost entirely commercial due to regulatory (21 CFR Part 11, GDPR, etc.), validation, and scale requirements. Open foundations exist via **OpenClinica**, ePRO/eCOA research tools, and related open clinical research stacks. This section expands those while remaining realistic about the commercial gap.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms
- **[Medable](https://www.medable.com/)**  
  Decentralized clinical trial platform covering eConsent, telehealth, eCOA, patient communication, and related engagement workflows.

- **[THREAD Research](https://www.threadresearch.com/)**  
  Decentralized trial software for eConsent, telehealth, eCOA, data collection, and participant communication.

- **[Science 37](https://www.science37.com/)**  
  Decentralized clinical trial operating system and patient engagement capabilities for remote and hybrid studies.

- **[Signant Health](https://www.signanthealth.com/)**  
  eCOA, eConsent, connected devices, and patient support tools for clinical outcome assessment and engagement.

- **[Clario](https://clario.com/)**  
  Endpoint and eCOA technology supporting patient-reported and clinician-reported outcomes in clinical trials.

- **[Castor](https://www.castoredc.com/)**  
  Clinical trial platform with eConsent, ePRO/eCOA, and decentralized trial features for sponsors and sites.

- **[MyTrials](https://www.mytrials.com/)**  
  Clinical trial and patient engagement solutions supporting study participation workflows.

- **[ObvioHealth](https://www.obviohealth.com/)**  
  Decentralized and digital clinical trial platform focused on patient-centric data capture and engagement.

- **[Reify Health](https://www.reifyhealth.com/)**  
  Clinical trial technology including recruitment and site/patient coordination tools that support engagement.

- **[YPrime](https://www.yprime.com/)**  
  eCOA and clinical technology platform for electronic outcomes and related patient-facing trial tools.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects
- **[OpenClinica](https://github.com/OpenClinica/OpenClinica)**  
  Open-source electronic data capture (EDC) and clinical data management system used for studies, eCRFs, and related clinical workflows.

- **[REDCap](https://projectredcap.org/)**  
  Widely used research electronic data capture platform (institutional licensing model) frequently extended for ePRO and survey-style patient engagement.

- **[ODK / Open Data Kit](https://github.com/getodk)**  
  Open-source mobile data collection tools adaptable for field and participant-reported data in research settings.

- **[Arcwell and open clinical research platforms](https://arcwell.health/)**  
  Open-source oriented clinical research platforms exploring eConsent, eCOA/ePRO, and participant-facing tools.

- **[FHIR Questionnaire / PRO open implementations](https://github.com/)**  
  Open libraries and SMART apps for structured patient-reported outcomes using FHIR standards.

- **[eConsent open prototypes](https://github.com/)**  
  Research and community projects for electronic informed consent flows and audit trails.

- **[Clinical survey and diary open tools](https://github.com/)**  
  Lightweight open survey engines usable for simple ePRO diaries in non-regulated research.

- **[Documentation and open clinical research playbooks](https://github.com/)**  
  Guides for deploying open EDC, ePRO, and participant communication in academic and low-resource trials.

- **[Self-hosted EDC + messaging stacks](https://github.com/)**  
  Patterns combining OpenClinica or similar with open messaging for basic engagement workflows.

- **[Open quality and outcome measure libraries](https://github.com/)**  
  Shared libraries of validated instruments that can be delivered via open questionnaire tools.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options
- Using **OpenClinica** or institutional **REDCap** for structured data capture and simple participant surveys.
- Delivering PROs via open FHIR Questionnaire apps in research contexts.
- Accepting that validated eCOA, full DCT stacks, telehealth integration, participant payments, global scale, and regulatory compliance still require commercial platforms (Medable, THREAD, Signant Health, Clario, Science 37, Castor, etc.).
- Focusing open-source efforts on standards-based instruments, academic trials, and transparent data capture logic.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Capture outcomes with OpenClinica/REDCap or FHIR questionnaires → manage consent with open eConsent prototypes → communicate via open messaging → retain audit logs. Suitable for academic and non-registrational research. Sponsored registrational trials almost always use commercial clinical engagement platforms.
